How market pickup works

  1. Choose your broth and the number of jars you want.
  2. In Your Cart, choose a market and a listed pickup date, enter your name and email, and review the total.
  3. Submit your order. It is recorded only after the secure server accepts it. We'll have it ready at the market and date you selected; bring your order reference and keep your private receipt link.
  4. Choose the payment option shown at checkout. If you choose Pay at Pickup, payment is due at pickup when you collect your order at the market.

Only dates shown for a market are available. These steps describe the normal process once ordering is available.

Monthly pickup is a one-time order. It never renews or repeats, and you are never charged again. If plans change, you can move the order once, to one alternative market, from your private receipt until your pickup window closes. The new pickup can be up to 7 days after your original date, and the server confirms the new market, date and window.
