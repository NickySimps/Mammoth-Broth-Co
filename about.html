Our Story

Food with a history, made for the way we gather now.

Mammoth Broth Co. is inspired by ancestral cooking methods and the intention of building a deeper connection to our food, our tribe, and our health.

Sourced from local, regenerative farms and slow simmered for 48 hours, our broth is a reliable source of high quality fuel in the modern world.

Why Bone Broth?

  • Flavorful taste!
  • Supports immune system
  • Supports gut health & digestion
  • Supports healthy bones, joints, & skin
  • Naturally contains collagen, minerals, aminos & electrolytes

Ways to Enjoy

  • Heat and sip
  • Rice, quinoa, lentils
  • Soups and stews
  • Use fat for cooking
  • Dogs love it too
  • Ramen & pasta
  • Mashed potatoes
  • Sauces & gravies
  • Fasting
  • Healing & recovery

Storing Your Broth

  • Keep frozen or refrigerated
  • Use within 7 days after thawing
  • Use ice trays for easy portioning

Serving Your Broth

  • Thaw under hot water
  • Pour desired amount into vessel
  • Use to cook or heat and sip
  • Store frozen portion for later
  • If sipping, stir well!
